## Essence
Inventive problems contain a contradiction: improving X worsens Y (cheap vs premium, powerful vs simple, scale vs quality). Ordinary analysis compromises between them; inventive analysis DISSOLVES the contradiction — separating the demands in time, space, condition, or system level. The interrogation: find the contradiction the draft never named, and refuse the compromise until dissolution was attempted.

## Interrogation set
1. State the central contradiction: improving ___ worsens ___. If the draft contains no tension, it is optimizing, not innovating — is that sufficient for the ask?
2. Can the two demands be separated in TIME (different demands at different moments — freemium is this), SPACE (different segments/channels), or CONDITION (different rules above a threshold)?
3. Can the contradiction move UP a system level — solved by the platform/ecosystem rather than the product? (Marketplace solves supply vs curation this way.)
4. What is the Ideal Final Result — the outcome achieved with zero added cost/complexity? How close does an existing resource get you?
5. Which industry solved this exact contradiction already, and what did their solution look like transplanted here?
6. Is the draft's "balanced" recommendation actually the worst of both worlds?

## How to apply
- Force the contradiction into one sentence before any solutioning; vague tension = weak analysis.
- Weak answer smell: "we'll balance affordability and quality"; premium features bolted onto a budget positioning.
- Does NOT apply: pure sizing/landscape questions with no design decision.

## Output contract
Revision must name the governing contradiction, show at least two dissolution attempts (time/space/condition/level), and justify any remaining compromise as genuinely irreducible.
