## Essence
A QA spine for strengthening work without dictating how it was created. Assess whether the output is complete, rigorous and decision-useful; convert observations into a SMALL set of located failure modes; prescribe the minimum intervention that fixes each. Never rebuild by default.

## Interrogation set
1. Coverage — walk the lenses Boundary, State, Structure, Actors, Mechanism, Drivers, Constraints, Dynamics: which materially relevant dimension is absent, and which absent dimension is *deliberately and defensibly* omitted?
2. Reasoning — is the objective framed correctly? Where are facts and assumptions mixed in the same sentence?
3. Causality — for each conclusion: what is the causal mechanism, and was it tested or merely asserted?
4. Narrative — does the draft progress context → insight → implication → decision? Which section fails to earn the next?
5. Traceability — pick the top recommendation: can you walk recommendation ← opportunity ← implication ← insight ← evidence without a broken link?
6. Levers — is the opportunity universe broad enough, or did the draft anchor on the first viable option?

## How to apply
- Diagnose max 6 failure modes, each typed: coverage / evidence / causal / synthesis / storyline / lever-completeness gap. CRITICAL only if it could change the conclusion.
- Format per finding: *Critical gap → Why it matters → Augment (minimum intervention)*.
- Weak answer smell: generic scores ("evidence 3/5") instead of located gaps; feedback that amounts to "rewrite it my way".
- Does NOT apply: when the ask is a quick factual lookup — do not manufacture gaps.

## Output contract
Revision must address every CRITICAL finding, state which omissions are accepted-as-immaterial and why, and stop when remaining gaps are immaterial to the objective (iterate, don't gold-plate).
