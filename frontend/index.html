Agent Council — market research, sharpened by review

A 4-agent AI market-research council. Scout turns your ask and uploaded files into a research brief; Astra drafts the analysis with live web search; Vera — a demanding first-principles reviewer, with an optional Expert Mode that retrieves task-specific strategy-framework lenses (Wardley, Porter, unit economics, premortem and 16 more) — and Cleo, a commercially-minded client reviewer, interrogate the draft; Astra folds every critique into the final analysis. Reports render tables and mermaid diagrams and export to a styled PDF (Typst engine) or PPTX deck. Runs persist server-side and survive page reloads. Bring your own Claude, GPT, or Hugging Face API key — keys are request-scoped, never stored.
